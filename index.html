cm

cm

Used to calculate the volume & liters dynamically inside the dashboard and ESP32 (Arpico 10K L is ~228cm).

tanks

Defines how many identical, parallel-connected tanks are in use. This scales the total volume and mass shown on the dashboard.

seconds

Configures how frequently (in seconds) the ESP32 publishes MQTT telemetry and performs HTTP Background Logging (e.g. 15 seconds).
